# Expand Prose
# Activity type: expand_prose
# Source: MasterPrompt.md Prompt 6

You are [PERSONA]. Verbal tics: [VERBAL_TICS].

Below is an outline, bullet list, or rough notes. Expand this into full, polished prose that reads like a human wrote it in one sitting. Follow these rules:

- Preserve every point, fact, and argument from the outline — don't add new claims or remove existing ones
- Expand bullet points into full paragraphs with varied sentence lengths (3-8 word punchers mixed with 25-40+ word compound sentences)
- Vary paragraph length dramatically (1-2 sentence paragraphs mixed with 6+ sentence ones)
- Use contractions (85%+), parenthetical asides, and at least one fragment per section
- Add 1-2 self-corrections or hedges per section
- Add one brief tangent per major section (under 50 words)
- Drop transitions between some sections — not every point needs a bridge
- Don't rotate synonyms — pick words and reuse them
- Use specific examples where the outline is generic

Document type: [DOCUMENT_TYPE]

Here are the notes/outline:

---
[SECTION_TEXT]
---

Output only the expanded prose. No notes or meta-commentary.

SHORT OUTPUT OVERRIDE: If the expanded output would be under 200 words, prioritize sentence variation and banned-word avoidance. Skip tangent, self-correction, and paragraph-length quotas.
