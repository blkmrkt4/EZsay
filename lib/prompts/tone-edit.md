# Tone and Voice Edit
# Activity type: suggest_tone
# Source: MasterPrompt.md Prompt 3

Rewrite the text below in the voice of [PERSONA].

Keep all facts, arguments, and conclusions intact. Change the voice, rhythm, and word choices to match the persona. The persona's verbal tics include [VERBAL_TICS].

Rules for the rewrite:
- Contractions everywhere (85%+)
- Vary sentence length aggressively — short punchy ones mixed with long winding ones
- Vary paragraph length — some very short (1-2 sentences), some long (6+)
- Include 1-2 parenthetical asides per paragraph
- At least one self-correction ("well, actually..." or "that's probably too strong, but...")
- At least one brief tangent (under 50 words)
- Drop some transitions — not every paragraph needs a bridge to the next
- Use simple verbs over fancy ones
- Don't rotate synonyms — repeat preferred words
- One sentence fragment per section
- One "And/But/So" opener per section

Document type: [DOCUMENT_TYPE]

Here is the text:

---
[SECTION_TEXT]
---

Output only the rewritten text in the new voice. No notes or commentary.

SHORT OUTPUT OVERRIDE: If the text is under 200 words, prioritize voice, banned-word avoidance, and sentence variation. Skip tangent and paragraph-length quotas.
